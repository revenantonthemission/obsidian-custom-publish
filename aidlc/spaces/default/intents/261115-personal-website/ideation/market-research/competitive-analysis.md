# Competitive Analysis — Passion Project personal website

Upstream: `ideation/intent-capture/intent-statement.md` (problem, three audiences, requested capabilities, success metrics).

## Comparison Set

The comparison set is software-engineer personal websites in general; no specific sites were named (market-research Q1). No site was surveyed for this document, so every statement about "typical" sites below is a **hypothesis** drawn from common patterns, not a measured finding.

## What Visitors Expect (Table Stakes)

| Expectation | Status | Source |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| A list of all writing with dates and topics | Confirmed as the standard visitors expect | Q2 |
| Who you are and how to contact you | Requested in the intent (About, Contact and links), but not named as a visitor expectation | Intent statement; Q2 |
| Projects with what you did and the outcome | Requested in the intent (Projects showcase), but not named as a visitor expectation | Intent statement; Q2 |
| A current "Now" status | Requested in the intent and chosen as a convention to follow | Intent statement; Q5 |

## Typical Patterns on Engineer Personal Sites (hypothesis)

| Pattern | Typical form (hypothesis) | Passion Project today | After this work |
|---------|---------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Homepage | Short introduction, then links to work and writing | A vault note made of topic link lists | A site-built landing page (intent statement) |
| Writing | Reverse-chronological list, often with tags | Tags page and graph; no single list of all posts | A Writing section by date and topic (intent statement) |
| About | A biography page | Profile slot on the homepage; résumé and portfolio pages | An About page (intent statement) |
| Projects | Cards linking to repositories or case studies | Role-based portfolio pages | A Projects showcase with detail pages (intent statement) |
| Contact | Email plus social profile links | Not grouped in one place | Contact and links (intent statement) |
| Now | A short "what I'm doing now" page | None | A Now page (intent statement; Q5) |

## Differentiators

| Differentiator | Why it stands out | Source |
|----------------|-------------------|--------|
| Connected notes: Graph, hub pages and backlinks | Most personal sites present posts as a flat list (hypothesis); the existing site already links notes to each other | Q3 |
| A strict Apple Human Interface Guidelines look and feel | A consistent, platform-quality visual language is uncommon on personal sites (hypothesis) | Q3; intent statement |
| Depth of the Korean computer-science and engineering writing | Long-form Korean writing across CS, engineering and linguistics topics | Q3 |

## Positioning

For recruiters, engineers and friends (intent statement), Passion Project is a personal website whose writing is connected like a knowledge base and whose design follows Apple's HIG, so a visitor can both understand the owner quickly and explore a deep body of Korean technical writing (Q3).

## Gaps Against the Comparison Set

- No single list of all writing, which is the one table-stakes item confirmed (Q2; intent statement problem).
- The connected-notes differentiator only helps if visitors can enter it from the new Writing section; today Tags and Graph sit in the top bar (intent statement).
