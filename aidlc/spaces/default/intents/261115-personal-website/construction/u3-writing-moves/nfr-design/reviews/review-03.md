### Summary

All four prior findings are fixed and checked against the source and the built tree. The design is implementable. The main open gap is a Major one: the 301's caching headers are never specified, so the claimed rollback does not undo the redirect for returning visitors. Two Minors are open: the writer does not apply the server's grammar before writing, and the inline-budget guard does not say which `<style>` element it reads.
