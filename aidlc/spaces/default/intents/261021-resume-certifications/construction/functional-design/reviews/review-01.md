### Summary

The design is mostly implementable. The model choice is sound: a second `credentials` collection carries section membership in the structure and keeps the PDF render-once rule intact. The r9 and E16 flow fits the existing approval chain, and every rule traces to a requirement. Of the requirements review's two Major findings, the model rule is resolved by the collection and the publication order is resolved as a recommendation. The two Majors above are hidden code-level breaks that Code Generation would hit: the manifest's six-section cap with its live-order check, and the hyphen-only parsers for displayed period text in the PDF layers. They can be fixed by revising the spec at the gate.
