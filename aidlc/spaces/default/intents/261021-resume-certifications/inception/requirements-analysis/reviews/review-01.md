### Summary

The requirements are mostly complete and testable. They trace to the request and to the answers Q1–Q7, and they handle fact approval, TDD, accessibility and the PDF candidate well. Two Major gaps should be fixed at or just after approval, and neither blocks engineering. The first is the understated certification model: issuer, link and section assignment. The second is the unspecified order between promoting the PDF and the nightly web deploy. The rest are Minor.
