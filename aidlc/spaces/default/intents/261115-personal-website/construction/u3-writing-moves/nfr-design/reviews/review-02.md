### Summary

All ten prior findings now hold: the dotted-key contradiction is routed through a declared amendment with exact wording, the acceptance grammar is testable, the publish window is closed by a server-side guard instead of by an atomic publish, and the two performance measurements are stated in terms that survive content hashes. Four minor points remain — a grammar that does not cover two of the rejections its own evidence list promises, a post-deploy check with no runner, an inline-budget paragraph naming an option this Astro version does not have, and a fallback answer that is not always 404 — none of which changes the shape of the build, but each should be settled before the design is implemented word for word.
