### Summary

The intent is well grounded. Problems, audiences, trigger, decision ownership and the boundary all trace to confirmed answers, and nothing has been invented about stakeholders. The main weakness is that success is measured only by writing coverage and HIG compliance, not by the job-search outcome that triggered the work. The vault-authoring choice also leaves open questions about the publish folder, telling pages from posts, and a second copy of project facts. These should be visible before approval, but they do not block it.
