### Summary

The performance and security targets are mostly concrete and correctly reflect the two Major findings carried from functional design (`NFR6.9`, Fixtures row). The main weaknesses are traceability and baselines. Requirements sit under the wrong inception NFR, so NFR4 (deterministic output) and NFR5 (fail-closed) show as covered when U2 has no requirement for them. NFR1.8 depends on a baseline and a measurement that do not exist. Both are fixable within this stage without redesign. Each remaining check (the script comparison page, page weight, build time, the CSS budget, the escaping test method, the glass fallback) needs a tighter pass/fail definition.
