### Summary

The design is coherent in its main shape — one build-written table, one request-time lookup, one shared address module — but five gaps would force a developer to guess or to rework: the dotted-key decision contradicts frozen NFR5.25, NFR6.16 and the tech-stack decision while traceability marks them met; the table's acceptance grammar does not say how a `//host` target is classified; the nightly `rsync -a --delete` publish is not atomic, so a 301 can point at a 404 during the deploy; and two performance measurements (the hashed-asset claim and the fixed expected request list) do not survive contact with Astro's content-hashed filenames.
