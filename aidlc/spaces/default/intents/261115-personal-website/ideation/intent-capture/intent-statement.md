# Intent Statement — Passion Project personal website

## Problem Statement

- Visitors cannot quickly tell who the owner is and what they do [Q1]
- The writing has no single list of all posts, so it is hard to browse [Q1]
- Projects are only visible through the role-based portfolio [Q1]
- The blog has outgrown a homepage made of link lists [Q4]

## Target Customer

| Visitor | What they need from the site | Source |
|---------|------------------------------|--------|
| Recruiters and hiring managers | To evaluate the owner: who they are, current work, projects, résumé and how to reach them | [Q2] [desc] |
| Engineers and readers | To find and browse the writing, by date and by topic | [Q2] [desc] |
| Friends, classmates and communities | To see who the owner is and what they are doing now | [Q2] [desc] |

## Requested Capabilities

- A site-built landing homepage showing who the owner is, current work, featured projects and the latest writing [desc]
- A Writing section: an index of all posts by date and topic, with Tags and Graph under it [desc]
- An About page [desc]
- Contact and links: email, GitHub, LinkedIn and RSS [desc]
- A Projects showcase with project detail pages, beyond the role-based portfolio [desc]
- A Now page [desc]
- The whole site strictly follows Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, with Lucide icons tuned to SF Symbols weight [desc]
- About, Now and Projects content is written in the Obsidian vault and published by the nightly run [Q5]
- The new homepage replaces the current homepage note ("Passion Project.md") entirely [Q6]

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Pass criterion | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Writing coverage | Every published post is reachable from the Writing section | [Q3] |
| HIG and accessibility | Every new page passes the existing Apple HIG and accessibility checks (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA) in light and dark | [Q3] |

## Initiative Trigger

- The owner is preparing for a job search or career move [Q4]
- The blog has outgrown a homepage made of link lists [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected scope: `feature` [scope]
- User-confirmed product boundary: the first version leaves out an English translation; the site stays Korean [Q9]

None.
