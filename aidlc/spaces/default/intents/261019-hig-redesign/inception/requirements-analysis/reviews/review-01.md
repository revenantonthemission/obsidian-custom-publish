### Summary

The requirements are strong on traceability, Q&A fidelity and testable acceptance criteria for most areas. Three gaps would cause rework or a disputed outcome: the cleanup requirements contradict each other on deleting the scaffolding branch, old appearance state in returning readers' browsers is not handled, and contrast on translucent materials has no workable pass/fail test. Fix these and the remaining Minor items, and engineering can start without coming back with questions.
